## 🎯 Learning Outcomes — What You WILL Be Able to Do

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. **Set up** a professional Meta Business Portfolio, Facebook Page, and Ads Manager account correctly
2. **Launch** boosted posts and full ad campaigns targeted to the right audience, location, and budget
3. **Choose** the right objective (Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, Sales) for any business goal
4. **Track** results with Meta Pixel / Conversions API and read your data like a professional
5. **Reduce** cost-per-result by testing creatives, audiences, and placements
6. **Manage** ad accounts for clients — and get paid for it

## 👥 Who This Course Is For

- Small business owners promoting their own products
- Freelancers who want to offer ad management services
- Social media handlers of shops, schools, and brands
- Content creators growing their pages and reach

## 📚 Course Modules

### Module 1: Foundation Setup (Do It Right From Day One)

**Outcome:** A correctly structured business account that won't get restricted.

- How Meta Ads Work: Auction, Budget, and Delivery
- Facebook Page Optimization for Business
- Creating a Meta Business Portfolio (Business Manager)
- Ads Manager Tour: Campaign → Ad Set → Ad structure
- Adding Payment Methods (cards, common payment issues)
- Ad Policies: Why Accounts Get Banned & How to Stay Safe
- Boost Post vs Ads Manager: What's the Real Difference?

### Module 2: Boosting That Works

**Outcome:** Boost posts that reach real potential customers, not random people.

- When Boosting Is Enough (and when it isn't)
- Choosing the Right Post to Boost
- Audience Targeting: Location, Age, Gender, Interests, Behaviors
- Setting Budget & Duration (how much is enough?)
- Advantage+ Audience: letting Meta's AI find buyers
- Reading Boost Results: Reach, Engagement, Cost per Result
- 🧪 **Live Practice:** Boost a real post with a real budget

### Module 3: Full Campaigns in Ads Manager

**Outcome:** Launch professional campaigns for any business objective.

- The 6 Campaign Objectives Explained with Examples
- Lead Generation Campaigns with Instant Forms
- Traffic & Sales Campaigns (WhatsApp, Website, Messenger)
- Ad Formats: Image, Video, Carousel, Reels Ads, Stories Ads
- Ad Copywriting: Hooks, Offers, and Call-to-Actions That Convert
- Creative Best Practices (sizes, text, thumb-stopping visuals)
- Placements: Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, Audience Network
- 🧪 **Live Practice:** Launch a lead generation campaign

### Module 4: Tracking, Optimization & Scaling

**Outcome:** Know exactly what's working and lower your costs.

- Meta Pixel & Conversions API: Setup and Verification
- Custom Audiences: Retargeting Website Visitors & Page Engagers
- Lookalike Audiences: Cloning Your Best Customers
- A/B Testing Creatives and Audiences
- Reading Reports: CPM, CPC, CTR, ROAS, Frequency
- When to Kill an Ad vs When to Scale It
- Common Mistakes That Burn Budget
- 🧪 **Live Practice:** Retarget your own website visitors

### Module 5: Client Work & Business

**Outcome:** Turn this skill into income.

- Managing Client Ad Accounts Safely (Business Portfolio access)
- How Much to Charge for Ad Management
- Monthly Reporting Template for Clients
- Getting Your First 3 Clients
