# 📖 Open Syllabus

> **Free, open-source, outcome-based syllabi for the world's most in-demand skills — built for teachers and students worldwide.**

`Open Syllabus` helps learners start learning and teachers start teaching — instantly. Every syllabus is designed to **international standards** with one obsession: **outcomes, not just output**. You don't just finish a course; you walk away able to *do* something real.

## ✨ Why These Syllabi Are Different

- **🎯 Outcome-Based:** Every module states what you will *be able to do* — not just what will be covered
- **🌍 International Standard:** Aligned with official exams (AWS CLF-C02, Google certifications) and industry practice
- **🛠️ Project-Driven:** Real labs, real campaigns, real capstone projects in every course
- **💼 Career-Focused:** Each course maps to specific job roles and freelance income paths
- **🆓 Free & Open Source:** Use it, teach with it, improve it
