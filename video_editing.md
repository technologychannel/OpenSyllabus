## 🎯 Learning Outcomes — What You WILL Be Able to Do

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. **Edit** complete videos — cuts, transitions, text, music, effects — confidently in industry tools
2. **Craft** stories that hold attention using pacing, hooks, and structure (the real skill of editing)
3. **Produce** platform-perfect content: YouTube videos, Shorts, Reels, and TikToks
4. **Fix** bad footage: color correction, audio cleanup, and stabilization
5. **Speed up** your workflow with AI tools: auto-captions, silence removal, AI voice, and upscaling
6. **Deliver** client projects with proper export settings, revisions, and pricing

## 👥 Who This Course Is For

- Aspiring YouTubers and content creators
- Freelancers who want high-paying editing clients
- Social media managers making video content
- Business owners creating their own promos

## 📚 Course Modules

### Module 1: Editing Foundations

**Outcome:** Understand the language of video before touching software.

- How Great Editors Think: Story First, Effects Second
- Video Fundamentals: Resolution, Frame Rate, Aspect Ratio, Bitrate
- Types of Cuts: Hard Cut, J-Cut, L-Cut, Jump Cut, Match Cut
- The Hook: Winning the First 3 Seconds
- Organizing Footage & Project Files Professionally
- Choosing Your Tool: CapCut vs DaVinci Resolve vs Premiere Pro

### Module 2: CapCut — Fast Content Creation

**Outcome:** Produce polished short-form videos quickly (mobile + desktop).

- CapCut Interface & Timeline Editing
- Trimming, Splitting, Speed Ramping
- Auto-Captions & Styled Subtitles
- Trending Effects, Transitions & Templates
- Keyframe Animation Basics
- Audio: Music, Sound Effects, Voiceover
- 🧪 **Project:** Edit 3 Reels/TikToks with hooks and captions

### Module 3: DaVinci Resolve — Professional Editing (Free)

**Outcome:** Full professional edits without paying for software.

- Resolve Interface: Media, Edit, Color, Fairlight, Deliver pages
- Multi-Track Timeline Editing
- Transitions and Text/Titles
- **Color Correction & Color Grading** (Resolve's superpower)
- Audio Mixing in Fairlight: Noise Reduction, Levels, EQ
- Green Screen (Chroma Key)
- Export Settings for Every Platform
- 🧪 **Project:** Edit a complete YouTube video (5+ minutes)

### Module 4: Adobe Premiere Pro — Industry Standard

**Outcome:** Work confidently in the tool most agencies and clients require.

- Premiere Interface & Sequence Settings
- Advanced Timeline Techniques & Shortcuts (edit 2x faster)
- Essential Graphics: Titles, Lower Thirds, Motion Presets
- Working with Multiple Cameras
- Warp Stabilizer, Speed Ramps, Masking
- Basic After Effects Integration (dynamic link)
- 🧪 **Project:** Edit a client-style promo video

### Module 5: AI-Powered Editing Workflow

**Outcome:** Cut your editing time in half with AI.

- Auto-Captions and Auto-Translate
- AI Silence & Filler-Word Removal
- Text-Based Editing (edit video like a document)
- AI Voiceovers & Voice Cleanup
- AI Image/Video Generation for B-Roll
- AI Thumbnails & Titles for Higher Clicks

### Module 6: The Business of Editing

**Outcome:** Turn your skill into income.

- Building a Portfolio Showreel
- Where to Find Clients (local businesses, YouTubers, Upwork/Fiverr)
- Pricing: Per Video vs Monthly Packages
- Handling Revisions Professionally
- Delivering Files & Managing Feedback
