## 🎯 Learning Outcomes — What You WILL Be Able to Do

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

1. **Build** a complete digital marketing strategy for any business — from local shop to online brand
2. **Rank** websites on Google using proven SEO techniques (keyword research, on-page, backlinks)
3. **Run** profitable ad campaigns on Meta and Google with measurable ROI
4. **Grow** social media accounts with content strategy, calendars, and analytics
5. **Convert** visitors into customers using email marketing and landing pages
6. **Measure** everything with Google Analytics 4 and present reports clients understand
7. **Use** AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, image/video generators) to work 10x faster

## 👥 Who This Course Is For

- Students & graduates seeking a high-demand career without coding
- Business owners who want customers from the internet
- Freelancers building a service business
- Marketing employees upgrading to digital skills

## 📚 Course Modules

### Module 1: Digital Marketing Foundations

**Outcome:** Think like a marketer — strategy before tactics.

- What is Digital Marketing? The Complete Landscape
- Traditional vs Digital Marketing
- The Marketing Funnel: Awareness → Interest → Desire → Action
- Understanding Your Customer: Buyer Personas
- Competitor Analysis
- Setting SMART Marketing Goals & KPIs
- 🧪 **Project:** Build a marketing strategy for a real local business

### Module 2: Content Marketing & Copywriting

**Outcome:** Create content people actually want to read, watch, and share.

- Content Strategy & Content Pillars
- Copywriting Formulas: AIDA, PAS, 4Ps
- Writing Headlines, Captions, and CTAs That Convert
- Blogging for Business
- Storytelling for Brands
- Content Calendar Creation
- AI-Assisted Content Creation (ChatGPT, Claude) — prompts that work

### Module 3: Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

**Outcome:** Get free, ongoing traffic from Google.

- How Search Engines Work (Crawling, Indexing, Ranking)
- Keyword Research (free & paid tools)
- On-Page SEO: Titles, Meta Descriptions, Headers, Internal Links
- Technical SEO Basics: Speed, Mobile, Sitemaps, Search Console
- Off-Page SEO: Backlinks That Matter
- Local SEO: Google Business Profile Mastery
- SEO in the AI Era: Answer Engines & AI Overviews
- 🧪 **Project:** Optimize and rank a real webpage

### Module 4: Social Media Marketing

**Outcome:** Grow and manage social channels professionally.

- Platform Strategy: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn
- Organic Growth: Reels, Stories, and Short-Form Video
- Community Management & Engagement
- Social Media Analytics & Reporting
- Influencer & Creator Collaborations
- Scheduling Tools & Workflow Automation

### Module 5: Paid Advertising (Meta + Google)

**Outcome:** Spend ad money and get more back.

- Meta Ads: Objectives, Targeting, Pixel, Retargeting *(deep-dive in our Facebook Boosting course)*
- Google Ads: Search, Performance Max, Demand Gen *(deep-dive in our Google Ads course)*
- Budgeting & Bidding Strategy
- Landing Pages That Convert
- Measuring ROAS Across Platforms
- 🧪 **Project:** Launch one Meta and one Google campaign

### Module 6: Email & Messaging Marketing

**Outcome:** Build an audience you own — no algorithm can take it away.

- Building an Email List (Lead Magnets)
- Email Tools Setup (Mailchimp / Brevo)
- Welcome Sequences & Newsletters
- Automation & Drip Campaigns
- WhatsApp & Messenger Marketing
- Open Rates, Click Rates, and Deliverability

### Module 7: Analytics & Reporting

**Outcome:** Prove your results with data.

- Google Analytics 4: Setup and Key Reports
- UTM Tracking for Campaigns
- Conversion Tracking Across Channels
- Building Client Reports (Looker Studio)
- Data-Driven Decisions: Double Down or Shut Down

### Module 8: Freelancing & Career Launch

**Outcome:** Get hired or get clients.

- Building Your Marketing Portfolio
- Pricing Your Services
- Finding Clients (Local + Upwork/Fiverr)
- Agency vs Freelance vs In-House Careers
- Interview Preparation
