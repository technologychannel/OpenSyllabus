## 📚 Course Modules

### Module 1: Google Ads Foundations

**Outcome:** Understand the auction and set up a healthy account.

- How Google Ads Works: The Ad Auction, Ad Rank & Quality Score
- Google Ads vs Meta Ads: Intent vs Interruption
- Account Setup the Right Way (avoiding "Smart Mode" traps)
- Account Structure: Campaign → Ad Group → Ads & Keywords
- Billing Setup & Budget Planning
- The 2026 Campaign Types Overview: Search, PMax, Demand Gen, Shopping, Display, Video, App, AI Max

### Module 2: Search Campaigns — The Core Skill

**Outcome:** Launch profitable search campaigns for any business.

- Keyword Research with Keyword Planner
- Match Types: Broad, Phrase, Exact — and When to Use Each
- Negative Keywords: Stop Paying for Wrong Clicks
- Writing Responsive Search Ads That Get Clicked
- Assets (Extensions): Sitelinks, Callouts, Calls, Locations
- Location, Language & Schedule Targeting
- Bidding Strategies: Manual CPC → Maximize Conversions → Target CPA/ROAS
- 🧪 **Live Practice:** Launch a real Search campaign

### Module 3: Conversion Tracking & Analytics

**Outcome:** Measure exactly which clicks turn into money.

- Why Campaigns Fail Without Conversion Tracking
- Google Tag Setup on Your Website
- Conversion Actions: Purchases, Leads, Calls, Form Fills
- Linking Google Analytics 4 with Google Ads
- Enhanced Conversions & Consent Basics
- Reading Reports: CTR, CPC, Conversion Rate, Cost/Conversion
- 🧪 **Live Practice:** Set up and verify conversion tracking

### Module 4: AI-Powered Campaigns (2026 Power Pack)

**Outcome:** Use Google's AI campaigns without losing control of budget.

- **Performance Max:** Asset Groups, Audience Signals, Reaching Search + YouTube + Display + Gmail + Maps from one campaign
- **Demand Gen:** Visual campaigns on YouTube, Discover & Gmail for new audiences
- **AI Max for Search:** What it is and when to trust it
- The Power Pack Strategy: How PMax + Search + Demand Gen Work Together
- Feeding the AI: Creative Assets & First-Party Data
- Controlling AI Campaigns: Exclusions, Brand Safety, Budget Guardrails
- 🧪 **Live Practice:** Build a Performance Max campaign

### Module 5: YouTube & Display Advertising

**Outcome:** Add video and banner reach to your campaigns.

- YouTube Ad Formats: Skippable, In-Feed, Shorts Ads, Bumpers
- Video Creative Basics That Stop the Skip
- Display Campaigns & Remarketing Audiences
- Frequency, Viewability & Brand Safety

### Module 6: Optimization & Client Management

**Outcome:** Improve results week after week — and get paid for it.

- The Weekly Optimization Routine (search terms, bids, budgets, ads)
- Improving Quality Score Step by Step
- A/B Testing Ads and Landing Pages
- Scaling Winners, Cutting Losers
- Client Reporting with Looker Studio
- Getting Google Ads Certified (free official exam walkthrough)
- Pricing Your Ad Management Services
